Professional summary

Dennis B. Paus teaches electronics with a student-centred approach covering analogue and digital circuits, communications, embedded systems, and IoT. His academic work includes curriculum development, laboratory instruction, assessment, project supervision, and the use of educational technology.

A Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Engineering at Mapúa University is in progress and has not yet been awarded. The research focus is embedded systems, TinyML, the Internet of Things, and AI-driven electronics.

The current appointment at Salalah Vocational College is a Ministry-supervised vocational post. It comprises teaching and laboratory instruction, and is distinct from a university faculty appointment. Higher-education lecturing experience was gained at Far Eastern University Institute of Technology.

Research statement

Doctoral work in progress at Mapúa University. The degree has not yet been awarded.

On-node machine learning for wireless sensor networks

Doctoral research addresses on-node machine learning for wireless sensor networks: running TinyML inference on microcontrollers with under 512 KB of SRAM, so that nodes transmit compact alerts rather than raw waveforms and batteries last years rather than days. The application domain is asset integrity: structural health monitoring and industrial predictive maintenance.
